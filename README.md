# HR Analytics Dashboard

## About the Project

This is a Power BI dashboard created to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends. It helps understand employee turnover based on age, salary, education, job role, gender, and years at the company using interactive charts and KPIs.

I built this project as part of my Power BI learning journey to improve my dashboard development and data visualization skills.

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across different factors
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition
- Understand the impact of age, salary, and education on attrition
- Present HR data in an interactive dashboard

## Dashboard Features

- Overall Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Years at Company
- Attrition by Education
- Attrition by Age
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Job Role
- Department Filters

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

## Key Insights

- Employees aged **26–35** had the highest attrition.
- Employees with salaries **up to 5K** showed the highest attrition.
- **Laboratory Technicians** recorded the highest attrition among job roles.
- Employees with **Life Sciences** education had the highest attrition.
- Male employees had higher attrition than female employees.
